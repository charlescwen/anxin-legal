Anxin Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026 Last updated: September 21, 2026

Who we are

Anxin (安心) is a companion app for older adults and their families, operated by Chi Wen ("we", "us"). Contact: tsctinc@gmail.com.

Anxin is not a medical device, not a doctor, and not an emergency service. It does not diagnose or treat anything. In an emergency call 911 (or your local emergency number).

What we collect, and why

We collect only what the app needs to work. Everything below belongs to your account and is used to run the app and to personalize it. We do not sell your information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. There are no advertising or analytics tools in the app.

What Why
Account details: your name, and the email address or phone number you sign in with (and your password if you use email, stored only in protected form by our sign-in provider) To sign you in and to show your name
Profile answers you choose to give: language, age, interests, dietary preferences, city or country you type, your choice about faith content, how you like Anxin to talk To personalize what Anxin says and suggests. All optional.
Conversations: what you say (voice recordings, turned into text) and type, Anxin's replies, short summaries, and the important things Anxin remembers (family, likes, life events) To let Anxin talk with you and remember you
Health-related words you volunteer (for example that you slept badly) They can appear in conversations and, only if you allow it, as an everyday "topic mentioned" in a family summary. We do not analyse them for medical purposes.
Daily check-ins, alerts and activity: when you check in, that you opened the daily devotional, and alerts Anxin raises when a message may need help So your family can see you are well, and so an emergency message is noticed
Family contacts you save: name, phone number and optional photo you pick So you can call family with one tap
Photos you choose for family contacts Same, stored privately
Family and sharing settings: who supports you, what each person may see, and a log of who looked To make sharing safe and auditable
Subscription records (if a family member buys a plan): which plan, for whom, dates, status and price, and Apple's transaction number. We never see card details. To give you the plan that was bought
Usage counts (how many chats and how much AI use per day) To apply daily limits and understand running costs

We do not use your device's location, we do not read your contacts (you pick one at a time through Apple's picker), and we do not use the camera. The microphone is used only while you are talking to Anxin.

Artificial intelligence and OpenAI

To chat, understand your voice, read answers aloud and write life tips, Anxin sends the following to our AI provider, OpenAI: your voice recording or typed text, recent chat, the things Anxin remembers and chat summaries, your language and faith-content choice, and (for a life tip) the age, interests, dietary preferences and city you entered. Before this happens the first time, the app asks your permission and names OpenAI. You can say no, and you can withdraw permission at any time in Me → AI & privacy (Settings on the elder version). Without permission, AI features stop and the rest of the app keeps working.

OpenAI may keep this information for a limited time to prevent misuse. We ask it not to store replies otherwise. Please see OpenAI's own privacy terms for how it handles data.

Family members and supporters

An older person can be supported by family members (called supporters). You decide what they see, and you can change it or end it at any time.

  • By default a supporter sees emergency alerts and daily check-ins (when you checked in, roughly how long you talked with Anxin, and whether you heard the daily devotional).
  • A supporter sees short everyday topics you mentioned (for example "mentioned not sleeping well") only if you turn on summaries for them.
  • No one in your family can read your conversations or the memories Anxin keeps about you. This is enforced by the database, not only by the app.
  • Every time a supporter looks at your information it is recorded. The app does not yet show you this list; you can ask us for it at tsctinc@gmail.com.

Sharing a phone. Two people can use Anxin on one phone. Each has their own chats, memory and settings, and the app asks who is using it. Both are on one login, so this separates them but is not a lock: anyone holding the phone can choose either person. Deleting the account that runs the phone deletes everyone added on it.

Who else handles your data

We use service providers who process data for us and are not allowed to use it for their own purposes:

  • Supabase: hosting and database (our project is in Canada). Stores your account and app data.
  • OpenAI: artificial intelligence, as described above.
  • Twilio: sends the sign-in code by text message (your phone number).
  • Apple: takes payment for subscriptions bought in the app and reports the purchase to us.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your information until you delete your account. You can delete your account in the app (Me → Delete my account, or Settings → Delete my account on the elder version). Deletion is immediate and removes your account and everything stored for you, including everyone added on your phone. For safety, you must have signed in within the last 10 minutes.

What deletion cannot reach: a subscription bought through Apple keeps billing until you cancel it in iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions (the app tells you before you delete). Our providers may keep limited records for a period under their own rules (for example OpenAI for misuse prevention, Twilio for message logs, Apple for payment records they must keep by law). Deleted data can remain in our backups until they expire.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and access to each person's data is limited by rules in the database so one account cannot read another's. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise it. Keep your device and sign-in details private.

Your choices and rights

You can view and correct your details in the app, withdraw AI permission, change what family sees, remove people from your phone, and delete your account. To ask anything else about your information, contact tsctinc@gmail.com. Depending on where you live you may have further rights; we will respond as the law requires.

Children

Anxin is intended for adults. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it applies. The date above shows the latest version.

安心 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 21 日　最后更新：2026 年 9 月 21 日

我们是谁

安心（Anxin）是为长者和家人设计的陪伴应用，由Chi Wen（“我们”）运营。联系邮箱：tsctinc@gmail.com。

安心不是医疗设备，不是医生，也不是急救服务，不做诊断或治疗。遇到紧急情况，请拨打 911（或当地急救电话）。

我们收集什么，为什么

我们只收集应用运作所需的内容，全部属于您的账户，只用于运行和个性化安心。我们不出售您的资料，不用于广告，也不会在其他公司的应用或网站上追踪您。 应用内没有广告或数据分析工具。

  • 账户资料：姓名，以及您用来登录的邮箱或手机号码（邮箱密码只以受保护的形式由登录服务商保存）——用于登录和显示您的称呼。
  • 您愿意提供的个人资料：语言、年龄、兴趣、饮食习惯、您填写的城市或国家、是否愿意谈信仰内容、希望安心怎样和您聊天——用于个性化，全部可选。
  • 对话：您说的话（录音会转成文字）和输入的文字、安心的回答、简短摘要，以及安心记住的重要事情（家人、喜好、生活大事）——让安心能和您聊天并记得您。
  • 您主动说出的与健康有关的话（例如睡得不好）：可能出现在对话中；只有您允许时，才会以“提到过某个日常话题”的形式出现在家人摘要里。我们不会把它用于任何医疗分析。
  • 每日报平安、提醒和活动：您何时报平安、是否听了今日灵修，以及安心在您的话可能需要帮助时发出的提醒——让家人知道您平安，也让紧急情况被注意到。
  • 您保存的家人联系人：姓名、电话和您选的照片——让您一键联系家人（照片私密保存）。
  • 家人与分享设置：谁在支持您、每个人能看到什么，以及谁查看过的记录。
  • 订阅记录（如果家人购买方案）：方案、对象、日期、状态、价格和 Apple 的交易编号。我们看不到银行卡资料。
  • 使用次数：每天聊天和 AI 使用量——用于执行每日限额、了解运营成本。

我们不会使用您的设备定位，不读取您的通讯录（您通过苹果的选择器一次选一位），不使用相机。麦克风只在您和安心说话时使用。

人工智能与 OpenAI

为了聊天、听懂您的话、朗读回答和写生活小贴士，安心会把以下内容发送给我们的 AI 服务商 OpenAI：您的录音或输入的文字、最近的聊天、安心记住的事情和聊天摘要、您的语言和信仰内容选择，以及（生成小贴士时）您填写的年龄、兴趣、饮食习惯和城市。首次使用前，应用会先征求您的同意并写明 OpenAI。您可以拒绝，也可以随时在“我的 → AI 与隐私”（长辈版在“设置”）收回同意；没有同意时 AI 功能停止，其他功能照常使用。

OpenAI 可能在一段有限的时间内保留这些内容以防止滥用，我们已要求它不要另外保存回答。请参阅 OpenAI 自己的隐私条款。

家人与支持者

长者可以有家人作为“支持者”。由您决定他们能看到什么，也可以随时更改或结束。

  • 默认情况下，支持者可以看到紧急提醒和每日报平安（您何时报平安、大约和安心聊了多久、是否听了今日灵修）。
  • 只有在您打开“摘要”时，支持者才会看到您提到过的日常话题（例如“提到睡眠不好”）。
  • 家人永远不能阅读您的聊天内容，也看不到安心为您记住的事情。 这是数据库层面的限制，而不只是应用的设置。
  • 支持者每次查看您的资料都会被记录。应用目前还不能显示这份记录，您可以发邮件到 tsctinc@gmail.com 向我们索取。

共用手机。 两个人可以在同一部手机上使用安心。每个人有自己的聊天、记忆和设置，应用会询问现在是谁在使用。但两人共用一个登录，所以这只是把两人分开，并不是锁：拿着手机的人可以选择任何一位。删除运行这部手机的账户，会同时删除在这部手机上添加的所有人。

还有谁会处理您的资料

我们使用为我们处理资料、不得用于自身目的的服务商：Supabase（托管与数据库，我们的项目位于加拿大）；OpenAI（人工智能，见上）；Twilio（发送登录短信验证码，涉及您的手机号码）；Apple（处理应用内订阅付款并通知我们）。如法律要求，或为保护他人安全，我们可能披露资料。

保存多久，如何删除

在您删除账户前我们会一直保存。您可以在应用内删除账户（“我的 → 删除我的账户”，长辈版在“设置 → 删除我的账户”）。删除立即生效，会删除您的账户和为您保存的一切，包括在您手机上添加的所有人。为了安全，需要您在 10 分钟内登录过。

删除无法做到的：通过 Apple 购买的订阅在您取消前会继续扣费，请在 iPhone“设置 → Apple ID → 订阅”取消（删除前应用会提醒您）。我们的服务商可能按各自规定在一段时间内保留有限的记录（例如 OpenAI 用于防止滥用、Twilio 的短信记录、Apple 依法必须保留的付款记录）。已删除的资料在备份过期前可能仍存在于备份中。

安全

资料在传输中加密，数据库规则限制每个人只能访问自己的资料。没有任何系统绝对安全，我们无法作出保证。请保护好您的设备和登录信息。

您的选择和权利

您可以在应用内查看和更正资料、收回 AI 同意、更改家人能看到的内容、从手机上移除人员、删除账户。其他关于您资料的问题，请联系 tsctinc@gmail.com。视您所在地区，您可能还有其他权利，我们会依法回应。

儿童

安心面向成年人，不面向儿童，我们不会有意收集18 岁以下儿童的资料。

变更

如政策有重要变更，我们会在应用内先行通知。上面的日期为最新版本日期。
