Anxin Support

Anxin (安心) is a companion app for older adults and their families. It is not a medical device or an emergency service. In an emergency call 911 (or your local emergency number).

Contact: tsctinc@gmail.com. We aim to reply within a few business days.

Common questions

I did not get a sign-in code. Check the phone number, wait a minute and try again. You can also sign in with email and password on the "Email" tab. Use "Forgot password?" to reset a password.

How do I delete my account? In the app: Me → Delete my account (Settings → Delete my account in the older-person version). Deletion is immediate and removes everything stored for you. A subscription bought through Apple keeps billing until you cancel it in iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

How do I cancel or manage a subscription? iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Anxin. In the app, "Restore purchases" is on the plan screen.

Who can see my conversations? No one in your family. Supporters see only what you choose to share (emergency alerts and daily check-ins by default). See the Privacy Policy.

Two people, one phone. Anxin asks who is using it and keeps each person's chats and memory separate.

常见问题

没有收到登录验证码？ 请检查手机号码，稍等一分钟再试；也可以在“邮箱”标签用邮箱和密码登录，忘记密码可点“忘记密码”。

如何删除账户？ 应用内：我的 → 删除我的账户（长辈版：设置 → 删除我的账户）。删除立即生效，并清除为您保存的所有资料。通过 Apple 购买的订阅需要您在 iPhone“设置 → Apple ID → 订阅”中取消，否则会继续扣费。

如何取消或管理订阅？ iPhone“设置 → Apple ID → 订阅 → 安心”。应用内方案页面有“恢复购买”。

谁能看到我的对话？ 家人看不到。支持者只能看到您选择分享的内容（默认是紧急提醒和每日签到）。详见隐私政策。

两个人共用一部手机？ 安心会询问当前是谁在使用，并分别保存各自的对话和记忆。
